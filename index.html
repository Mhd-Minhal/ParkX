What is PARKX?

PARKX combines sensors, RFID-based identification, parking indicators and an application interface into one smart-parking concept. The goal is to reduce unnecessary searching for spaces and give users a clear view of parking availability.

Questions visitors may ask

We included the answers here so visitors can understand the project without needing a separate explanation.

What problem is PARKX trying to solve?

PARKX addresses the difficulty of finding and managing available parking spaces. The idea is to reduce unnecessary searching by showing which spaces are free and by organizing entry and exit.

How many parking slots does your project have?

Our application prototype monitors three parking slots, identified as P01, P02 and P03.

How does the system know whether a slot is occupied?

A real implementation can use a suitable parking sensor, such as an ultrasonic or other presence sensor, to detect a vehicle. The sensor information is then used by the controller/application to update the slot status.

What is RFID and why are you using it?

RFID stands for Radio Frequency Identification. It allows a reader to identify an RFID tag without physical contact. In PARKX, it can be used for vehicle or user identification at controlled entry and exit points.

Does RFID itself detect whether a parking slot is empty?

No. RFID is mainly for identification. A parking sensor can determine whether a space is occupied, while RFID can identify the vehicle or authorized user.

What happens when all three slots are occupied?

The system can display that the parking area is full, for example “0 / 3 available”. A future version could direct the driver to another nearby parking area.

Can PARKX be expanded to a large parking lot?

Yes. More sensors, slot identifiers, RFID readers where required, and software capacity can be added. The exact hardware and communication design would depend on the size of the facility.

What are the main benefits?

The concept can reduce the time spent searching for parking, provide clearer parking information, support controlled access, and make parking status easier for users and operators to monitor.

Is PARKX a complete commercial parking system?

No. It is a science-fair prototype demonstrating the concept. A commercial system would need additional engineering, testing, security, reliability measures and integration with real parking infrastructure.

What could be improved in the future?

Future versions could support more slots, reservations, navigation to a selected slot, notifications, payment integration, cloud monitoring, analytics and stronger authentication/security.

Why is an app useful if there are indicator lights?

Lights help people already inside or near the parking area. An app can provide the same information remotely, allowing users or operators to see parking status before or while approaching the facility.

What is the main idea of PARKX in one sentence?

PARKX uses connected technology to make parking availability and access easier to detect, understand and manage.
