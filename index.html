What is PARKX?

PARKX combines sensors, RFID-based identification, parking indicators and an application interface into one smart-parking concept. The goal is to reduce unnecessary searching for spaces and give users a clear view of parking availability.

Built for smarter parking.

The prototype demonstrates the core ideas behind an intelligent parking system.

◉

Live slot status

Parking spaces can be represented as available or occupied so users can quickly understand the current situation.

⌁

RFID access

RFID technology can be used to identify authorized vehicles and automate controlled entry and exit.

↗

Clear guidance

Entry, exit and slot indicators make the parking area easier to navigate and demonstrate at a glance.
